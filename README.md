Privacy Policy for Submit-It

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Submit-It is an assignment tracking application designed to help students organize their courses, assignments, and due dates. Your privacy is important to us, and Submit-It is designed to function without collecting or transmitting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Submit-It does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share your personal data.

Information you enter into Submit-It, including:

* Courses
* Assignments
* Assignment descriptions
* Due dates and times
* Assignment completion status

is stored locally on your device.

The developer does not have access to this information.

2. Local Data Storage

Submit-It stores application data locally on your device to provide its assignment-tracking functionality.

Your assignment and course information is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by Submit-It or its developer.

Because the developer does not receive or maintain this information, the developer cannot access, retrieve, or remotely delete your locally stored data.

You can delete information directly within the app. Uninstalling Submit-It will remove data stored by the app from your device, subject to any backup or restoration functionality provided by your device or operating system.

3. Notifications

Submit-It may use local notifications to remind you about assignments and upcoming due dates.

These notifications are scheduled and generated on your device. Submit-It does not operate an external notification server that receives your assignment information.

You can disable notifications for Submit-It at any time through your device’s notification settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Submit-It does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services to collect information about you or your use of the app.

Submit-It does not sell or share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

5. Tracking and Advertising

Submit-It does not track users across apps or websites.

Submit-It does not display targeted advertising and does not use your information for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Submit-It does not collect or store your personal data on external servers, the developer does not retain copies of the courses, assignments, due dates, or other information you enter into the app.

Locally stored information can be removed using the app’s available deletion features. Uninstalling the app will remove locally stored app data from your device, subject to any backup or restoration functionality provided by your device or operating system.

Because the developer does not possess this locally stored information, there is no server-side account or personal-data record for the developer to delete.

7. Data Security

Submit-It is designed to minimize privacy risks by keeping assignment and course information locally on your device rather than transmitting it to servers operated by the developer.

However, no device or software system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should maintain appropriate security protections for their devices, such as device passcodes and other security features provided by their operating system.

8. Children’s Privacy

Submit-It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the app does not transmit personal information to the developer.

Information entered into the app remains locally on the user’s device as described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Submit-It’s features or data practices change.

If the policy is updated, the revised version will be made available at the Privacy Policy URL associated with Submit-It, and the effective date above will be updated.

If a future version of Submit-It introduces features that collect, transmit, or share user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
