# Privacy Policy for Submit-It

**Effective Date: August 19, 2026**

Submit-It is an assignment and reminder tracking application designed to help users organize courses, assignments, due dates, and general reminders.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Submit-It. Submit-It is designed to operate without accounts, an external database, or developer-operated servers. Information entered into the app remains locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

## 1. Information Handled by Submit-It

Submit-It does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share your personal information with the developer, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Information you enter into Submit-It may include:

* Course names and related course information
* Assignment titles and descriptions
* Assignment due dates and times
* General reminder titles and descriptions
* Reminder dates and times
* Notification preferences and reminder frequencies
* Assignment submission status
* Reminder completion status

This information is stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive, view, or have access to the contents of your courses, assignments, or reminders.

You are responsible for deciding what information to enter into the app. You should avoid entering highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable storing that information on your device.

## 2. Local Data Storage

Submit-It stores application information locally on your device to provide its assignment-tracking and reminder functionality.

Submit-It does not transmit or store your assignments, courses, reminders, descriptions, due dates, or completion information on servers operated by the developer.

Because the developer does not receive or maintain this information, the developer cannot access, retrieve, restore, modify, or remotely delete it.

You can remove information using the deletion features available within the app. Uninstalling Submit-It will ordinarily remove information stored locally by the app. However, copies of app data may remain in device backups or may later be restored through backup, migration, synchronization, or restoration features provided by Apple, your operating system, or your device.

The developer does not control device backups, operating-system restoration features, or data retained independently by Apple or another device-platform provider.

## 3. Notifications

Submit-It uses local notifications to remind users about assignments, upcoming due dates, and general reminders. Users may select when and how frequently certain notifications occur.

Notifications are scheduled and generated locally by your device. Submit-It does not operate an external notification server and does not transmit the contents of your assignments or reminders to the developer for notification delivery.

Submit-It may request permission to display notifications. You can allow, deny, or later disable notification access through your device settings.

Disabling notifications may prevent reminders from appearing, but it does not automatically delete assignments, reminders, or other information stored within the app.

Notification delivery is controlled in part by your device and operating system. Delivery may be affected by notification permissions, Focus modes, battery settings, system limitations, device configuration, software updates, or other circumstances outside the developer’s control. Submit-It does not guarantee that every notification will be delivered at an exact time.

## 4. Device Permissions

Submit-It may request access to device features that are reasonably necessary to provide its functionality, including permission to display notifications.

Submit-It does not use notification permission to access unrelated information on your device.

If additional permissions or features are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated where required to explain how they are used.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Submit-It does not include third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking services used by the developer to collect information about you.

Submit-It does not sell or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies.

Your use of Submit-It remains subject to the independent terms, privacy practices, and technical operation of Apple, the App Store, iOS, your device manufacturer, and any backup or device services you choose to enable. The developer does not control the information independently processed by those services.

## 6. Tracking and Advertising

Submit-It does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Submit-It does not display targeted advertising and does not use information entered into the app for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Submit-It does not sell personal information.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not retain server-side copies of information entered into Submit-It because the app does not transmit that information to developer-operated servers.

Locally stored information remains on your device until you:

* Delete it using the app’s available deletion features;
* Clear or reset the app’s local data, where supported by your device;
* Restore or erase your device; or
* Uninstall Submit-It.

Deleted information may remain temporarily or subsequently reappear if it exists in a device backup or is restored through a service controlled by your operating-system or device provider.

Because Submit-It does not require an account and the developer does not possess your locally stored information, there is no developer-operated account or server-side personal-data record for the developer to retrieve or delete.

## 8. Data Security

Submit-It is designed to reduce privacy risks by keeping course, assignment, and reminder information locally on your device instead of transmitting it to developer-operated servers.

However, no device, application, storage method, or software system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining reasonable protections for your device, including a device passcode, biometric authentication, current operating-system updates, and other security features made available by your device provider.

If another person has access to your unlocked device, device backups, or device account, that person may be able to access information stored by Submit-It.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

Submit-It is a general-purpose organizational application and is not specifically directed to children.

The developer does not knowingly receive or collect personal information from children through Submit-It. Information entered into the app remains locally on the user’s device and is not transmitted to the developer as described in this Privacy Policy.

Parents or guardians who permit a child to use Submit-It should supervise the information the child enters into the app and the security of the device on which the app is installed.

## 10. International Use

Submit-It may be available to users in multiple countries and regions. Because information entered into the app remains locally on the user’s device and is not transmitted to developer-operated servers, the developer does not intentionally transfer that information between countries.

Information may nevertheless be processed or stored by Apple, device backup services, operating-system services, or other services independently selected or enabled by the user. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Submit-It’s functionality, data practices, legal obligations, or platform requirements change.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be made available through the Privacy Policy URL associated with Submit-It, and the effective date at the beginning of the policy will be updated.

If a future version of Submit-It begins collecting, transmitting, sharing, or storing information outside the user’s device, this Privacy Policy will be revised to describe those practices before or when the relevant feature is made available, as required by applicable law.
